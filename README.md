# GRAVIX Tires & Wheels

**Grip. Performance. Confidence.**

A high-performance automotive storefront concept with **71 catalog items** across tires, wheels, complete packages, TPMS, accessories and garage equipment.

## Highlights
- Car, crossover, SUV and truck tires
- All-season, summer, winter, performance, all-terrain and mud-terrain selections
- Alloy, black, gunmetal, bronze and performance wheels
- Wheel sizes from 17 to 24 inches in the demo catalog
- Tire sizes ranging from everyday passenger fitments to truck/off-road examples
- Tire & wheel packages
- TPMS and tire accessories
- Garage tools and care products
- Vehicle fitment center
- Search, size, finish and category filters
- Product detail pages
- Wishlist and cart demo
- Complete-build collections
- Customer support
- Responsive design
- Local product photography
- GitHub Pages ready
